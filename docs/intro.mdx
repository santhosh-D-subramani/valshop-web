# Welcome to Val Shop

**Val Shop** is a powerful mobile companion app for VALORANT players, designed to give you complete visibility and control over your VALORANT experience on Android and iOS without needing to boot up your PC.

Whether you are away from home, commuting, or simply checking your rotation from bed, Val Shop connects directly to official Riot Games services to deliver your daily storefront, party management, social presence, progression tracking, and loadout customizer.

---

## Key Features at a Glance

* 🛍️ **Daily Store & Night Market**: View your active daily weapon skin offers, featured bundles, accessories, and Night Market discounts with live countdown timers.
* 🔔 **Wishlist & Background Alerts**: Add favorite skins to your wishlist and receive timely background notifications the moment they appear in your store rotation.
* 👥 **Party & Lobby Controls**: Check lobby status, change game modes (Competitive, Unrated, Swiftplay, etc.), select matchmaking server pods, toggle party privacy, and invite teammates with lobby codes.
* 💬 **Real-time XMPP Chat & Presence**: View live friend statuses (in-game scores, party size, menus) and send 1-on-1 messages through Riot's chat network.
* 🎯 **Missions & Battle Pass Progression**: Track daily checkpoints, active weeklies, future weekly XP projections, and agent recruitment contracts.
* 🎒 **Loadout & Inventory Browser**: Inspect your equipped weapon skins, chromas, buddies, player cards, sprays, and titles.
* 🔐 **Secure & Multi-Account Ready**: Sign in securely through Riot's official web login with full 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) support. Manage and switch between multiple accounts seamlessly.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Install the App**: Download Val Shop for Android from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.santhoshDsubramani.val_shop) (or build for iOS/Android from source).
2. **Sign In**: Tap **Sign In** and authenticate using your Riot Games account inside the secure in-app browser. Complete your 2FA verification if enabled.
3. **Explore Your Hub**: Once authenticated, your active store rotation, rank tier, Kingdom Credits balance, and active missions will load immediately.
