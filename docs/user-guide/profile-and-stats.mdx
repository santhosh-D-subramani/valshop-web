# Profile, Loadout & Match History

VThunai provides comprehensive tools to inspect your player profile, equipped inventory items, and recent match performance.

---

## Profile & Rank Goals

* **Player Overview**: Displays your current player level, account level progress, active player card banner, title, and current competitive tier (Rank & RR points).
* **Rank Goal Tracker**: Set custom rank milestones (e.g., reaching *Ascendant 1* or *Immortal*) and monitor required RR to reach your target division.

---

## Loadout Inspector

Browse and verify your currently equipped cosmetic inventory across all weapon slots:

* **Weapon Slots**: Sidearms (Classic, Shorty, Frenzy, Ghost, Sheriff), SMGs (Stinger, Spectre), Shotguns (Bucky, Judge), Rifles (Bulldog, Guardian, Phantom, Vandal), Snipers (Marshal, Outlaw, Operator), Heavies (Ares, Odin), and Melee weapons.
* **Chroma & Level Variants**: View the currently active skin chroma colorway, level upgrade tier, and attached Gun Buddy.
* **Accessories & Cards**: Inspect your equipped player card, title, and spray wheel layout.

---

## Recent Matches & XP Breakdown

* **Match Summaries**: Review your latest games with final match scores, map name, agent played, and game mode.
* **XP Gain Breakdown**: Inspect detailed XP breakdowns per match, categorizing XP earned from rounds won, time played, and completed daily/weekly missions.
