# Privacy & Security

Security and privacy are core architectural priorities of VThunai. We believe players should have full transparency regarding how their credentials and data are handled, and the entire codebase is open-source on [GitHub](https://github.com/santhosh-D-subramani/VThunai) for anyone to audit.

---

## Direct Client-to-Server Communication

* **Zero Middleman Servers**: VThunai operates strictly as a client application. All network requests for authentication, storefronts, match data, and XMPP chat are made **directly from your mobile device to official Riot Games servers** (`auth.riotgames.com`, `entitlements.auth.riotgames.com`, `pd.<shard>.a.pvp.net`, `glz.<shard>.a.pvp.net`, `*.chat.si.riotgames.com`).
* **No Telemetry of Credentials**: We do not collect, transmit, or store your passwords, session tokens, cookies, or personal account identifiers on any external third-party server.

---

## On-Device Storage

* **Local Preferences**: Your session tokens, serialized session cookies, and active user profile indexes are stored locally on your device's isolated application storage (`SharedPreferences`).
* **Account Deletion / Logout**: Logging out or deleting an account profile immediately purges all associated tokens, session cookies, and cached data from your local device storage.

---

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Support

VThunai fully supports Riot Games' Multi-Factor Authentication. We strongly recommend enabling 2FA on your Riot Account for maximum protection.
