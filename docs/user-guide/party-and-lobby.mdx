# Party & Matchmaking Lobby

Val Shop allows you to interact with your VALORANT party lobby in real-time, even when you're AFK or stepping away from your PC.

---

## Lobby Overview & Members

When connected, the **Party** tab queries Riot's GLZ endpoints to display your active lobby details:

* **Party Members**: See all players currently in your party, their Riot ID (Name + Tag), player card, competitive rank tier badge, ready state, and party leadership status.
* **Party Owner Controls**: If you are the party owner, administrative controls are unlocked allowing full lobby management.

---

## Matchmaking Controls

* **Queue Selection**: Change the party game mode instantly. Supported queues include:
  * **Competitive**
  * **Unrated**
  * **Swiftplay**
  * **Spike Rush**
  * **Deathmatch** / **Team Deathmatch**
  * **Custom Games**
* **Toggle Ready Status**: Toggle your ready state with a single tap to let your party leader know you are set.
* **Start / Cancel Matchmaking**: Party owners can initiate the match queue search or cancel matchmaking at any point directly from their phone.

---

## Server Selection & Game Pods

Val Shop dynamically parses available server pods and pings, letting you specify your preferred matchmaking regions:

* Select preferred regional servers (e.g. *Mumbai, Singapore, Tokyo, Frankfurt, London, Ashburn, California*).
* View localized city names resolved automatically from game pod configurations.

---

## Party Privacy & Invite Codes

* **Open / Closed Lobby**: Toggle party accessibility between `OPEN` (friends can freely join) and `CLOSED` (invite/request only).
* **Lobby Join Codes**: Generate or revoke a 5-digit lobby code that allows friends or community members to join your party directly.
