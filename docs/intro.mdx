# Welcome to VThunai

**VThunai** is a free, open-source mobile companion app for VALORANT players, built with Flutter for Android and iOS to give you complete visibility and control over your VALORANT experience without needing to boot up your PC.

Whether you are away from home, commuting, or simply checking your rotation from bed, VThunai connects directly to official Riot Games services to deliver your daily storefront, party management, social presence, progression tracking, and loadout customizer.

---

## Key Features at a Glance

* 🛍️ **Daily Store & Night Market**: View your active daily weapon skin offers, featured bundles, accessories, and Night Market discounts with live countdown timers.
* ⭐ **Wishlist & Favorites**: Add favorite skins to your wishlist and easily track them with highlighted in-app indicators when they appear in your daily store.
* 👥 **Party & Lobby Controls**: Check lobby status, change game modes (Competitive, Unrated, Swiftplay, etc.), select matchmaking server pods, toggle party privacy, and invite teammates with lobby codes.
* 💬 **Friends Presence & Chat (Beta / WIP)**: Experimental live friend status viewing and direct messaging over Riot's chat network (in active development).
* 🎯 **Missions & Battle Pass Progression**: Track daily checkpoints, active weeklies, future weekly XP projections, and agent recruitment contracts.
* 🎒 **Loadout & Inventory Browser**: Inspect your equipped weapon skins, chromas, buddies, player cards, sprays, and titles.
* 🔐 **Secure & Multi-Account Ready**: Sign in securely through Riot's official web login with full 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) support. Manage and switch between multiple accounts seamlessly.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Install or Build the App**: Download pre-built releases from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/santhosh-D-subramani/VThunai/releases) (including unsigned `.ipa` builds for iOS sideloading via AltStore or SideStore), or build for Android/iOS from source in the [VThunai GitHub repository](https://github.com/santhosh-D-subramani/VThunai).
2. **Sign In**: Tap **Sign In** and authenticate using your Riot Games account inside the secure in-app browser. Complete your 2FA verification if enabled.
3. **Explore Your Hub**: Once authenticated, your active store rotation, rank tier, Kingdom Credits balance, and active missions will load immediately.
