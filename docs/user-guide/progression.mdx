# Contracts, Missions & Progression

Track your daily checkpoints, weekly mission batches, Battle Pass tiers, and agent recruitment contracts with ease.

---

## Daily Checkpoints & Missions

* **Daily Checkpoints**: View your daily checkpoint completion rings (XP and Kingdom Credits earned for completing daily rounds).
* **Reset Timers**: Displays countdowns to the daily reset so you know when new checkpoints unlock.

---

## Weekly Missions & XP Projections

* **Active Weeklies**: View current weekly missions with numerical progress bars (e.g. *Use 200 Ultimate abilities: 142/200*) and XP reward values.
* **Upcoming Future Weeklies**: Plan your Battle Pass leveling in advance! Val Shop lists locked weekly mission sets grouped by their future unlock weeks, allowing you to calculate the total XP pool available before the season ends.

---

## Battle Pass & Event Passes

* **Act Progression**: Monitor your active Act Battle Pass level, Epilogue tiers, and total XP required to reach your target tiers.
* **Reward Visualizer**: Dynamic resolution of upcoming rewards (Weapon Skins, Player Cards, Gun Buddies, Titles, Radianite Points, Sprays) with high-definition preview images.
* **Event Pass**: When special seasonal event passes or community passes are active, track custom milestones and reward unlocks separately.

---

## Agent Recruitment & Kingdom Credits

* **Agent Contracts**: Monitor progress towards recruiting locked agents or unlocking Chapter 2 tier rewards (sidearm skins, player cards, sprays).
* **Kingdom Credits Wallet**: Check your Kingdom Credits balance (up to the 10,000 KC cap) and track upcoming KC earnings to plan your accessory purchases.
