# Storefront, Bundles & Wishlists

The Daily Shop is the core hub of Val Shop, allowing you to check current rotations, countdown timers, bundles, accessories, and bonus stores directly from your mobile device.

---

## Daily Store Rotation

Every 24 hours at 00:00 UTC (or your regional reset time), Riot Games generates a fresh rotation of 4 random individual weapon skins for your account.

* **High-Definition Previews**: Browse your 4 daily weapon skin offers with high-resolution artwork, content tier badges (Select, Deluxe, Premium, Exclusive, Ultra), and price tags in Valorant Points (VP).
* **Skin Inspection**: Tap any skin card to inspect its full details, chromas/color variants, level upgrade animations, finisher video demonstrations, and sound effects.
* **Live Reset Countdown**: A live, synchronized countdown timer displays the exact hours, minutes, and seconds remaining until the next shop rotation.

---

## Featured Bundles & Night Market

* **Featured Bundles**: View the current active premium bundle, bundle discount percentages, complete weapon lineups, gun buddies, player cards, sprays, and remaining bundle duration.
* **Accessory Store**: Check the weekly accessory storefront offering past Battle Pass player cards, gun buddies, titles, and sprays purchasable with **Kingdom Credits (KC)**.
* **Night Market (Bonus Store)**: When the Night Market event is active in VALORANT, Val Shop automatically displays your 6 mystery cards with their custom percentage discounts and VP prices.

---

## Wishlist & Favorites Tracking

Keep track of your most wanted weapon skins (such as the *Prime Vandal*, *Reaver Vandal*, or *Kuronami no Yaiba*) so you never overlook them when checking your daily rotation.

### How to Add Skins to Your Wishlist

1. Search for any weapon skin in the app or tap the **Heart icon** on any skin detail page.
2. The item is saved to your local Wishlist.

### In-App Wishlist Indicators & Highlights

* **Wishlist Screen**: Browse all your saved favorite skins in one dedicated screen, view their tier badges, and inspect their animations.
* **Storefront Match Banner**: When opening the Daily Shop, Val Shop compares your rotation against your saved favorites. If any matching skin is in your daily 4 offers, a prominent banner immediately alerts you inside the app.
